I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST AI and a research intern at NVIDIA. My research focuses on spatial and 4D understanding for vision–language models — equipping them with fine-grained motion perception (4DP-QA) and agentic spatial reasoning (SpatialClaw). This direction builds on my work on Track Any Point (point tracking) and its applications to 3D and 4D video understanding and reconstruction.

I have had the opportunity to intern at NVIDIA and Adobe, where I was honored to work with inspiring mentors, including Orazio Gallo, Abhishek Badki, Hang Su, and Jindong Jiang at NVIDIA, as well as Joon-Young Lee and Gabriel Huang at Adobe. I am currently working with Ryo Hachiuma and Min-Hung Chen at NVIDIA Research in Taiwan.
